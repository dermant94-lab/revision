### 1 · Pre-viva assessment of Part 2

**Verdict: needs major revision at two joints and at one pattern of statement; the rest is repair of proportion, pointer and attribution.** The three commitment sections are exact in their loci (nothing misquoted in 72 collated items; the single W3 is a hyphen in a title), and their best passages are among the strongest in the thesis: the bounded concession of P040 («what the argument establishes is negative»), the token/type limit and the two falsifiers of P052, the categorial holding-apart of P051, the distinction of inescapability from transcendental necessity at P034. #### P026 · C1 (L69; 290 words; nn. 63–64)

**Utterance 1.**
> one formulation is entered against a loss the reception has repeatedly incurred, and it is not Habermas's, that acknowledgement is an identity-condition upon the object of the reconstruction and not a warrant for its content, settling whose competence is described and nothing about whether the description is accurate or general
