### 2 · Pre-viva assessment of Part 1

**Verdict: needs major revision — of three inferences, one census, two hand-offs and the scope of the thesis's central term, not of the exegesis of Habermas's texts, which is careful and, at its best, exact.** Of the 213 spans collated not one is misquoted; six are correctly quoted and glossed against a qualification the source enters within two sentences, and two of those (P005, P023) bear weight. The head of §2.1 is the strongest piece of Habermasian scholarship in the fleet on post-metaphysical thinking as a dated situation, and §2.1.2's argument that the two tests of reconstructive science are blind to modality is the chapter's own and is valid as far as it goes. The defects are of five kinds. *Exegesis against the page:* P017 reads MCCA 95 as assigning the demonstration of performative contradictions to identification and not to corroboration, when MCCA 97 makes the search for performative contradictions the guide of step (2) as a whole and MCCA 95 makes the no-alternatives claim, (2c)'s own object, what the demonstration proves; P007 makes Habermas's two-limbed criterion 'the same' as Kant's at B xxxv, where Kant licenses apodictic science after critique; P005 extends the verdict of PMT 43 to Adorno, who is not among 'these attempts'. *Prejudgement of Chapter Three:* P007's second limb and P024's 'corrigibility is a property of the proposition as such' each classify Apel's position as dogmatic or excluded by definition, deciding in advance what the thesis says Chapter Three decides. *Scope of the central term:* P023 introduces the *Begründungsdefizit* for the second interval, the Introduction and the Glossary fix it for both, P025 and P028 use it for the first, and Habermas's own first use of the word (MkH 14 = MCCA 6) names the first interval and is unused. *Census:* the inventory of 'quasi-transcendental' at P023 omits three own-voice occurrences of 1973, 1974 and 1980 (VuE 180 n. 45, 233, 528), one of which predicates *nicht hintergehbar* of the validity basis of every speech act, and the reservation grid's falsifiability rests on two candidates where the fleet's own Glossary adduces a third. *Debt discipline:* P003, P008 and P009 promise §2.3 something §2.3 does not contain. ### 3 · Conceptual map of Part 1 (claims in the order of dependence)

The head of §2.1 fixes post-metaphysical thinking as a dated situation (P002), states what it renounces in three respects (P003), regroups the renunciations under three tenets whose Habermasian answers are the three commitments (P004), and shows the self-understanding to be self-critical in a determinate sense, the reflexive circle (P005); P006–P008 derive from the recoil of the critique of metaphysics the need for a criterion of manner, take it from Kant, transpose it to a practice in two limbs, and test it on Henrich, where it cannot decide; P009 separates the two critical tasks the criterion assigns and P010 installs reconstructive science as the discipline for which philosophy holds a place. §2.1.1 defines reconstruction against explanation and separates the locative from the epistemic thesis (P011), fixes the two levels and their one-directional answerability (P012), and places Apel's reflection outside the reconstructive sciences (P013). §2.1.2 carries the chapter's first thesis: from the hypothetical status of substeps (2b) and (2c) (P014–P016) to the blindness of recognition and counterexample to modality (P017) and the silence of reconstruction by construction (P018), which rests upon the demarcation of data and the three exclusions of §2.1.3 (P019–P020). §2.1.4 fixes the modality Habermas does assert, indexed five times across the dated series (P021–P022), and reserves the vocabulary in which the dispute with Apel is to be conducted, designating the deficit in anticipation (P023). §2.1.5 draws the consequence for the relation of theory to mode, nothing modal passing between enactment and description in either direction without a premise §2.2.2 will supply (P024), and locates the charge of provincialism (P025). Everything downstream depends on P017–P018 (the silence), P021–P023 (the indexed modality and the reservation), and P024 (the two directions); the head depends on nothing downstream and is depended upon, in the body, only for the sense of 'post-metaphysical' (P003–P004) and for the reflexive circle (P005 → §2.3.1).

#### P001 · Preamble (L5; 497 words)

**Utterance 1.**
> formal pragmatics requires a passage its own resources cannot supply, from what speakers enact, as reconstruction renders it, to the bindingness of what they certify

*Unearned modality; scope slide against the finding reported.* Severity 2. Chapter One `_38` closes §1.3.5 by finding that the third place is 'nowhere, upon the loci consulted above, shown to generate from its own resources' the passage, and that 'what is handed to Chapter Two is the joint and not a verdict' (T-verified, Chapter One `_38` L175). The preamble reports that located absence as an impossibility in the very sentence which says that Chapter One «declined to enter a verdict»; the two halves of the sentence contradict each other, and the stronger half is the one an examiner will quote. The chapter's own procedure forbids the upgrade: §2.1.2 argues that reconstruction cannot *tell* whether the passage can be made, which is a claim about the method's silence and not about the passage's impossibility, and §2.4 is careful to say so («that the presuppositions of argumentation are contingent, nor that they are necessary, but that reconstruction cannot tell which»). The opening must be no stronger than the close.

Replacement:
> formal pragmatics requires a passage which, upon the loci Chapter One consulted, its own resources are nowhere shown to supply, from what speakers enact, as reconstruction renders it, to the bindingness of what they certify

**Utterance 2.**
> it is the limit of post-metaphysical thinking visible from within it, none of the resources examined here addresses it, and that is why Chapter Three turns to the dispute between Apel and Habermas over the irrefutability of the presuppositions on which the validity of discourse is made to rest (§2.3.4, §2.4)

*Suppressed premise and a routing the chapter's own close contradicts.* Severity 2. The connective makes the aporia the reason for the turn to the irrefutability dispute. §2.4 sends to Chapter Three 'the first interval and the legitimacy question' and says in terms «To the Conclusion goes the aporia» (P082). The reason Chapter Three follows Chapter Two is the first interval, the modal silence of reconstruction meeting Apel's claim that a reflection of another kind can speak where the tests cannot; that is also the office the author fixed for Chapters One and Two with his supervisor on 27 September, as the ground of Apel's critique of the irrefutability of the presuppositions which Chapter Three conducts. The sentence also states the aporia without the condition §2.4 attaches to it, that its intelligibility 'rests upon the strong reading Chapter Three §3.3.2 argues for and leaves contestable' (P081); an opening which omits a proviso its closing declares claims more than the chapter earns. The self-marker «examined here» goes with the rewrite.

Replacement:
> it is the limit of post-metaphysical thinking visible from within it, upon a reading of its intelligibility which Chapter Three argues for and leaves open to contest, and none of the three resources addresses it (§2.3.4, §2.4). Chapter Three receives the first of the deficit's intervals, at which the silence of reconstruction upon modality meets Apel's claim that a reflection of another kind can speak where the tests cannot, and conducts on that ground the dispute over the irrefutability of the presuppositions on which the validity of discourse is made to rest.

**Utterance 3.**
> None of the three is resolved by weakening a commitment, since a programme which weakened one would no longer be the programme

*Equivocation on 'resolved'.* Severity 1. §2.2.3 states that each tension 'is resolved, while the programme's claim stands, only by weakening the commitment to which it is assigned' (P056), and §2.4 that none is resolved 'without surrendering the programme' (P081). The tensions *are* resolved by weakening a commitment; what is lost is the programme. The preamble should say what the chapter says.

Replacement:
> None of the three can be resolved by weakening a commitment without surrendering the programme, which the three commitments individuate

**Utterance 4.**
> the categorial premise on which the instrument rests being amended under Derrida's argument from iterability rather than defended unchanged

*Term before antecedent.* Severity 1. 'The instrument' has no antecedent; the next sentence names two instruments. The categorial premise is glossed only at §2.2.2, which is acceptable in a preamble that points, as this one does, but the pointer should be given.

Replacement:

#### P004 · §2.1 (L13; 407 words; nn. 10–13)

**Utterance 1.**
> Each tenet is the negative of one commitment.

*Contrary taken for contradictory.* Severity 2. The paragraph's own verbs name contraries ('displaces', 'forecloses', 'is the refusal of'), and the chapter's principal interlocutor is the counterexample: Apel, who coined 'methodological solipsism' as the chapter's own n. 10 records, rejects the first two tenets, holding that no position outside the language game is available (P034, P046), without holding reconstructivism or fallibilism in Habermas's sense, since he places the reflection he relies upon outside the reconstructive sciences (P013) and withholds fallibility from what claim-raising presupposes (§2.2.1). The rejection of a tenet does not yield the commitment; the commitment is one answer to the tenet. The consequence the paragraph draws (that the deficit is 'a finding about post-metaphysical thinking as Habermas practises it, and not about one theory conducted under it') survives only with the qualifier it already carries, and the logical relation stated must be no stronger than that qualifier allows, or the chapter presents one exit from the philosophy of consciousness as the only one.

Replacement:
> Each commitment is Habermas's answer to one tenet, though not the only answer the tenet admits: Apel's position, examined at §2.2.1, rejects the first two tenets without holding either reconstructivism or fallibilism in Habermas's sense.

**Utterance 2.**

*Suppressed premise, and a misassignment the paragraph itself records.* Severity 2. The one-to-one correlation credits to C1 a displacement the chapter argues under C2.

Replacement:
> and it deprives the first tenet of its object, since its datum is public, while the tenet's claim about the theorist's position is met by the participation requirement examined at C2

**Utterance 3.**
> The Introduction described the position post-metaphysical thinking vacates in three forms, a principle, a faculty and a standpoint, and the chapter regroups Habermas's renunciations under three tenets of the philosophy of consciousness whose negatives its three commitments are

Replacement of the closing clause «the three commitments are not additions to post-metaphysical thinking but the form it takes in a method» → 'the three commitments are not additions to post-metaphysical thinking but the form it takes in Habermas's method'.
